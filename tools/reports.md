# The STARCITY Papers

## Lincoln, measured from its own public data

Each paper below takes a public dataset the city or county already publishes and adds it up in a way that is hard to do by hand. Every one states its method and its limits. None names an owner or an address.

| Paper | What it finds | Read |
|---|---|---|
| **Floodplain exposure and flood loss** | 8,310 parcels and $5.64 billion of assessed value, 12.71% of the roll, sit in the mapped floodplain. | [Web](floodplain.html) · [PDF](Lincoln-Floodplain-Report.pdf) |
| **Who Lincoln over-assesses, and who it does not** | Assessments pass the level and consistency standards and fail the equity one: lower-priced homes are assessed at a higher share of sale price. | [Web](assessment.html) · [PDF](Lincoln-Assessment-Equity.pdf) |
| **Where Lincoln's property value lives** | A tenth of the parcel land holds 44% of the assessed value. Downtown is assessed at $6.9 million an acre. | [Web](taxbase.html) · [PDF](Lincoln-Property-Value.pdf) |
| **What sold in Lincoln** | 3,340 home sales in twelve months at a median of $320,000. Fewer than one in ten closed under $200,000. | [Web](sales.html) · [PDF](Lincoln-Home-Sales.pdf) |
| **Lincoln's TIF districts by the numbers** | 89 active projects hold $1.29 billion of excess value, about $22.9 million a year in property tax at the 2024 levy. | [Web](tif.html) · [PDF](Lincoln-TIF.pdf) |

The map these came from is at [starcitylincoln.org](index.html).
