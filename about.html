About STARCITY

Lincoln, Nebraska

Code for a more open and better informed society, starting in Lincoln, Nebraska.

Public information that arrives as a PDF or a flat chart gets rebuilt here so you can see it in 3D, interact with it, find your own place in it, and view it in ways that were not possible on paper.

What this is

STARCITY is a free 3D map of Lincoln, Nebraska, built entirely from public records. It is independent. It is not run by the city, the county, the university or any business, and it has no ads, sponsors or tracking.

Who made it

This is a personal project, done on my own time. It does not represent any employer.

Why

Public data belongs to the public, and most of it is hard to find and harder to read. Putting it on one map, on the real ground, lets anyone see their city the way planners and analysts do, and bring real numbers to a council meeting, a newsroom or a kitchen table.

How it is made

Every number here has a source and a date, and the code is open. The map is built with AI assistance, which I direct and check against the original records. The STARCITY Papers state their methods and limits in full. The verification log shows how each was checked and what was corrected.

What it will not do

It does not name private property owners, and it does not collect personal data from visitors.

Found a mistake, or want something added?
