What Sold in Lincoln

Lancaster County, Nebraska · November 21, 2026

Home sales, November 2025 to November 2026

Published November 21, 2026 by STARCITY, starcitylincoln.org. Data retrieved November 21, 2026 unless a section says otherwise.

Summary

The county's sales file holds 3,340 valid residential sales inside Lincoln's city limits for the twelve months ending in mid November 2026, worth $1.21 billion in total. Read as a whole they describe the market a buyer or seller walked into this year.

  1. The median home sold for $320,000, at a median of $251 per square foot. The middle half of sales fell between $250,000 and $425,000.
  2. The entry level is thin. 9.3% of sales closed under $200,000. 15.2% closed at $500,000 or more.
  3. New construction is a large share of the market. 348 sales, 10.4% of the total, were homes built in 2025 or 2026, at a median of $469,450.
  4. Price per square foot is flat across size until the largest homes. Every size group under about 1,792 square feet sold for $253 to $260 a square foot. The largest fifth sold for $216.
  5. Homes sold for more than their assessment. 83% of sales closed above the assessed value, by a median of $24,975.

Turnover

3,340 valid sales against 87,483 improved residential parcels is 3.8% of the housing stock changing hands over the period. The file counts only sales the county validated as arm's length, so the true count of deeds recorded is higher.

What these numbers are not

  • Not a forecast. This describes twelve months of closed sales.
  • Not every sale. Every record carries the county's validity code 01. Family transfers, foreclosures and other sales the county set aside are not in the file.
  • Not the listing market. Days on market, list prices and price cuts are not public record and are not here.
  • Not a full last quarter. Sales from October and November 2026 are still being posted.
  • Not about any one house. No addresses or owner names were used. Sales are grouped by type, age, size and neighborhood only.

What to do with it

If you are buying. The age and type tables show where the price points are. Under $250,000 the market is mostly houses built before 1960, bungalows and condominiums.

If you are selling or listing. Price per square foot by neighborhood and by type gives a first check on a list price. Remember that it is above-grade area.

If you are on the council. New construction is selling at a median near $469,000, and 9.3% of sales close under $200,000. The supply of homes at entry prices is the existing older stock, which is a reason to care what happens to it.

Method

Sales are the Lancaster County Assessor's published sales layer, 3,761 records countywide dated November 23, 2025 through November 16, 2026. This paper uses the 3,430 located inside Lincoln's corporate limit, of which 3,340 are residential. Price per square foot, floor area, structure type and year built are the county's fields. Neighborhood was assigned by matching each sale's parcel number to the 2026 parcel layer and locating that parcel inside the city's neighborhood association boundaries. The assessment comparison uses the assessed value carried on each sale record. A fuller treatment of assessed value against sale price is in the companion paper on assessment equity.
