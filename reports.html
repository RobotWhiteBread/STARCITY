The STARCITY Papers

Lincoln, Nebraska · November 21, 2026

Lincoln, measured from its own public data

Each paper below takes a public dataset the city or county already publishes and adds it up in a way that is hard to do by hand. Every one states its method and its limits. None names a property owner or an address. The campaign paper names donors and committees only as the state publishes them.
